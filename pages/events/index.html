ENGINEERING LADIES’ DAY (ELD)

Engineering Ladies Day is an annual faculty event aimed at bringing together all female students of the University of Lagos Engineering Society (ULES). this day focuses attention on the amazing careers in Engineering, and allows us to celebrate the female students, enlighten, empower and inspire them to grow up to be great innovators and problem solvers of the next generation. It consists of two sessions; morning educative session and dinner night. The morning session is most likely to hold at the New Engineering lecture Theatre (NELT) and the dinner at the schools Multi-Purpose Hall
