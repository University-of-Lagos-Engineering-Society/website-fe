FAQs

❮
Q

How can I volunteer for any ULES event?

Ans

There are Chairperson’s in charge of every ULES event, you can reach out to them and if it’s not possible, the ULES excos are there to assist you.

Q

Is engineering tough?

Ans

Everything depends on your mindset. If you believe it is tough that is what you will see it as and vice versa. But in all sincerity Engineering is not beans

Q

How do I become an exco?

Ans

To become an exco you must have spent 2 years as a student in the faculty and must meet a minimum CGPA of 3.5.

Q

Is everyone welcomed into the ULES office?

Ans

Everyone is welcomed to the ULES office to carry out anything official and ask questions they are not clear about in the faculty. The office hours are (9AM – 5PM).

Q

Do I need to visit my course adviser regularly?

Ans

Only when the need arises (for official reasons), which can be regular or irregular lol.

Q

Are there any sport activities?

Ans

Yes, there are. ULES creates an environment that allows maximum growth of the Unilag engineer, by organizing sporting activities aimed at improving unity and sportsmanship among ULESites.

Q

Is there a library in Engineering?

Ans

Yes, there is an Engineering Library. It is located in the department of Surveying and Geomatics. It is only opened to Engineering students.

Q

Can we meet the excos anytime?

Ans

Yes, you can. They are always ready to help you out

Q

Does ULES have any social media page?

Ans

Of course, we do! Please do follow us on our Instagram and Twitter. Also visit our website for the most up to date information as well as future calendars.
