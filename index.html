You Are Welcome

The University of Lagos Engineering Society (ULES), is the oldest student body in the University of Lagos. Since its inception, it has been led by various great personalities and I’m honored to be the sitting president. The faculty currently has 10 departments and with the help of my tenacious executives, we ensure that every ULESite has a smooth journey through out their stay in the faculty. The ULES is led by students from different departments across the faculty who were elected through a fair voting system. All work and no play would make us dull engineers, that is why we have numerous events to keep our prospective engineers active. Some of the events we organize annually are Engineering Career Expo (ECX), Engineering Ladies Day (ELD), Professor Ayodele Awojobi Design Competition (PAADC), ULES Orators and Writers, ULES Games and a host of other educative and fun events. These events help ensure maximum growth and development of the UNILAG ENGINEER. I hereby use this opportunity to welcome to you to the best faculty in the University of Lagos.

Hon. Adeyemi Jammal

ULES President (2019/2020 Session)
