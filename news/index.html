Vice Chancellor Commissions the Renovated Civil Shed

Koforowola Samuel • Writer & Managerial Editor

13th May, 2022

Last Friday kicked off with a big bang as our amiable Vice Chancellor, Professor Oluwatoyin Ogundipe unveiled the new Civil Shed. After two months of intense renovation, the previous 60-man sitter can now house 150 people comfortably, come rain or sunshine! (Hopefully, we don't face any storms soon).

The Dean of the Faculty, Professor __________, The Heads of Department of Systems, Elect./Elect., Mech and Civil (well, it's the civil shed) Engineering Departments and many more dignitaries graced the event with their presence and brought a few goodies (Excos only pls).

The Civil Shed now has dozens of charging spots for your phones and laptops (Please don't try to charge your rechargeable fans and remember to bring an extension if you must).

The Wifi Routers have also been upgraded to Wifi 5.0 (futuristic stuff!). Time to download those Udemy courses or play COD. If you're still playing PUBG, rest!

Whether it's to jack overnight or chill with your babe, this new Relaxation Shed really defines relaxation. We're grateful to TetFund and ASUU for this!

Check the shed out whenever you're in school. Peace!
